## Codex handover

Read docs/ZAISAKU-ART-CONSTITUTION.md, AGENTS.md and canonical references on the current integration branch. Continue existing development, do not start a parallel game. Review 80 illustrated concepts: 20 environmental structures, 20 utility props, 40 ground and micro-prop concepts (including variants and duplicates).

1. Preserve originals outside runtime. Extract sprites individually; remove captions, background, halo and baked shadow contamination. Do not assume grid crops are clean. Reject wrong perspective or generic decoration.
2. Match Mineral Analog, Vey/Oru/Sera, fixed angled top-down orthographic projection, 32px logical tiles and actual actor size. Define source size, display size, visual bounds, ground anchor, footprint, interaction target and layering independently.
3. Flat spills, moss, scratches, debris, markings and corrosion are non-solid, non-interactive ground overlays beneath actors. Grass and tall micro-props may be depth sorted; rodents are cosmetic and only animate if affordable. Do not introduce gameplay mechanics.
4. Place purposeful clusters: utility spills near machinery, parcel and tool details by workplaces, foliage along boundaries, wear near doors. Keep pedestrian approaches and negative space. No random uniform scattering; limit repeated motifs and light sources.
5. Separate canopy/passages into supports, visual overhang and collision. Reuse approved assets and existing rendering/data registries. Never replace superior existing sprites just to use new art. Keep dynamic typography and values out of sprites.
6. Validate dimensions, alpha, anchors, collision, joins when relevant and checksums. Track concept, technical, file-validated, in-game-validated and creator-approved states separately. Keep source art out of runtime bundles.
7. Test routes, NPC and door approaches, residence, save/reload, mobile and desktop rendering, frame time and reduced motion. Inspect actual screenshots against canonical references and iterate. No automatic merge into main.

Goal: subtle environmental density, coherent scale and believable everyday life on Zuisaku. These are source concepts, not 80 approved sprites.
